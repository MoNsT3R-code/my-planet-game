# Planet Pepper

## 🌐 Overview

Pepper runs errands on the smallest planet in the sky. Deliver parcels before they're late, round up runaway animals, find hidden stars and beat the robots in Hobby Park. Watch out for traffic: every bump costs a heart.

## ✨ Key Features

✅ **Five levels** - One tiny planet, with traffic that gets faster and busier each level.

✅ **Timed missions** - Parcel deliveries, runaway animals, hidden stars and Hobby Park minigames.

✅ **Combo scoring** - Grab coins and stars in quick succession to multiply your points.

✅ **Shop** - Spend coins on bike colours, outfits and hats.

✅ **Day and night** - A day and night cycle, a planet map, music and sound effects.

✅ **Saved progress** - Your high score, coins and best level are kept between sessions.

✅ **Phone support** - Play with an on-screen arrow pad.

## 🚀 How to Play

Clone or download this repository, then open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/MoNsT3R-code/my-planet-game.git
cd my-planet-game
# Open index.html in your browser
```

| --- | --- |
| Walk | `W` `A` `S` `D` or arrow keys, or click where you want to go |

| Switch day and night | `N` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

---

## 📜 Rules

- **Clear a level:** Finish every mission on the list before the clock runs out. The **gold arrow** points to your next goal.
- **Parcels:** Pick one up at the Post Office and drop it at the glowing building before its timer runs out.
- **Animals:** Runaways have a red **!** above them. Walk into one to catch it, then lead it back to the farm field.
- **Hearts:** Each hit with a car costs a heart. You start with three.
